## 🎯 What We Built

**Moon** is a comprehensive, next-generation web-based cataloging platform implementing BIBFRAME 2.0/3.0. It represents a complete enterprise solution for bibliographic description with advanced collaborative features, multi-institutional support, and cutting-edge ontology-driven architecture.

---

## ✨ Major Achievement: Load Real Example Button

### What Was Added

A **"Load Real Example"** button in the Moon Engine (Ontology-Driven cataloging template) that instantly populates the editor with complete, real-world BIBFRAME records.

### Why It Matters

- **Learning Tool:** New catalogers can see professional examples
- **Testing:** Explore all features with realistic data
- **Standards Compliance:** See how proper BIBFRAME/RDF looks
- **Time Saver:** No need to create test data manually

### Three Complete Real Examples Included

1. **One Hundred Years of Solitude** by Gabriel García Márquez
   - English fiction with complete LOC authorities
   - LCC/DDC classifications, subject headings
   - ISBN, LCCN, and full publication data

2. **الأيام (Al-Ayyam)** by طه حسين (Taha Hussein)
   - Arabic autobiography demonstrating RTL support
   - Arabic subjects, summaries, and metadata
   - Complete bilingual cataloging example

3. **Introduction to Algorithms** by Cormen et al.
   - Academic textbook with multiple authors
   - Edition statements, table of contents
   - Intended audience metadata
