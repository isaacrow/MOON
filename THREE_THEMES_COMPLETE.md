## 🎯 How to Switch Themes

### **Step 1: Open Moon Engine**
From dashboard → Click "Moon Engine"

### **Step 2: Click Theme Button**
Look at **top-right corner** → Click **"Theme"** button

### **Step 3: Choose Your Theme**
Dropdown appears with **THREE options**:

```
┌──────────────────────────────┐
```
