## 🎬 QUICK DEMO

### Create Your First Record (3 minutes)

```
STEP 1: Access Template
└── Dashboard → Click "🎯 Comprehensive Monograph Template"

STEP 2: Work Tab
└── Resource Type: Select "bf:Text"
└── Title: Enter "Introduction to Programming"
└── Classification:
    └── LCC: "QA76.6"
    └── DDC: "005.1"
└── Subject (Type to search):
    └── "Computer programming" (select from LOC results)
    └── "Algorithms" (select from LOC results)

STEP 3: Instance Tab
└── Title: "Introduction to Programming: Python Edition"
└── Publication:
    └── Place: "Boston"
    └── Publisher: "Tech Press"
    └── Date: "2024"
└── ISBN: "978-1-234567-89-0"
└── Extent: "350 pages"

STEP 4: Admin Tab
└── Cataloging Source: Your institution
└── Cataloger: Your name

STEP 5: Save & Export
└── Click "Save Record"
└── Click "Export JSON-LD" to download
```
