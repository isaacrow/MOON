## 🚀 Getting Started in 3 Minutes

### Step 1: Login (30 seconds)
Open Moon and login with your credentials

### Step 2: Access Moon Engine (30 seconds)
Click the **"Moon Engine"** card from the dashboard  
(Look for the ✨ Sparkles icon)

### Step 3: Load Example (30 seconds)
Click the green **"Load Real Example"** button  
Select any example to instantly populate the form

### Step 4: Explore (90 seconds)
- **Work Tab:** See intellectual content
- **Instance Tab:** See physical details
- **Preview Tab:** See JSON-LD, Turtle, MARC, OPAC

**You're now exploring a real BIBFRAME record!**
